# Data access and release scope

Only aggregate result CSVs reviewed for this manuscript are included. No raw patient images, participant-level records, per-image measurements, model checkpoints, segmentation caches, downloaded dataset archives, credentials or virtual environments are included.

The complete local Word manuscript and editable PowerPoint contain source dataset images. Their publication and redistribution permissions are not established in the current author-review draft. These binary documents and figure images are therefore withheld from GitHub. The complete text edition includes their captions, methods, equations and tables, with explicit artwork-withheld notices. Local originals are retained without removal of their figures.

| Resource | Release constraint |
| --- | --- |
| mBRSET | Credentialed access and data-use terms; the local mirror is not proof of redistribution rights |
| RAV | Local data include material beyond the unrestricted public subset; no full redistribution presumed |
| FIVES, Fundus-AVSeg, HRF | Obtain data from their providers and verify the particular version, attribution and redistribution terms |
| VascX and other weights | Obtain under original licenses; weights are not republished here |

This repository is a curated research archive, not a substitute for data-provider approval or an ethics exemption. Private visibility is not a redistribution license. Historical documents may mention local manifests or cached outputs that are deliberately absent from this release. The source study citations are in `paper/references/`.

Remaining release work: verify source-image permissions; settle code and model licensing; prepare authorized data-access instructions and a portable environment; decide whether to make an appropriately cleared subset public. No automatic future publication is scheduled.
