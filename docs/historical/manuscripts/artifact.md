## Page and document structure

- A4 portrait, one column.
- Margins: 1.25 inch left and right; 1.00 inch top and bottom.
- Sequence: title, authors, affiliations, corresponding author, Abstract, Keywords, numbered Introduction, Methodology, Results, Discussion, Limitations, Conclusion, Declarations, References.
- Figures span the full text width and are centred. Captions are placed below figures.
- Table captions are placed above tables.

## Typography

- Body: Times New Roman, 12 pt, justified, 1.15 line spacing, 6 pt after.
- Title: Times New Roman Bold, 16 pt, left aligned, 4 pt after.
- Authors, affiliation, and correspondence: Times New Roman, 16 pt.
- Main numbered headings: Times New Roman Bold, 14 pt, 10 pt before, 6 pt after.
- Numbered subsections: Times New Roman Bold, 12 pt, 10 pt before, 6 pt after.
- Figure and table captions: Times New Roman, 12 pt, justified; label and title lead are bold.
- Equations: native Word OMML, centred, Cambria Math, without equation numbers, matching the reference.
- References: Times New Roman 12 pt, justified, hanging indent, numbered Vancouver style.

## Tables

- Conventional scientific three line design.
- No vertical rules or shaded cells.
- Bold centred header row, top rule, header rule, and bottom rule only.
- Body text 12 pt; numerical columns centred.
- Rows must not split across pages; header repeats on continuation pages.

## Content constraints

- The template supplies layout and typography only. No RegionSuff scientific content is reused.
